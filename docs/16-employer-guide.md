# 16 - Employer guide (plain words)

For recruitment agents and companies who want to show a job ad on **Jobs Abroad**. You can give this page to your employers as it is.

## What you get
Job seekers in India see your ad for free, without logging in, and apply to you by WhatsApp or by a short form. **Posting ads is free.** To keep job seekers safe, only **verified** employers can post: you send your license once, our team checks it, and then you can post.

## 1. Make your account (free, 2 minutes)
1. Open the site and tap **Login**, then **Sign up**.
2. Write your **company name**, **phone number**, **registration number** (if you are a recruitment agent: this is very important), **email** and a **password** (at least 8 letters).
3. Tick the box to accept the **employer rules**. Tap **Create account**.
4. Open your email, find the message from us and tap the link to confirm. If you do not see it, look in the spam folder.
5. Now log in. You are on **My ads**.

Forgot your password? On the login page tap **Forgot password**, write your email and open the link we send. The link works once.

## 2. Get verified: send your license
Until you are verified you cannot post an ad. Tap **Post an ad** and you see **Your company must be verified first**.
1. Write your **license number**.
2. Add a clear **picture (JPG or PNG) or PDF of your license** (up to 3 MB). Nobody except our team can see it.
3. Tap **Send license**.
4. Our team checks that the license is real, in your company's name, and still valid. This can take a day. They may phone you.
5. If we cannot accept it, the screen tells you why. Fix it and tap **Send license again**.
6. When we verify you, a **Verified** tag shows on your account and you can post.

**If you change your company name or registration number, the Verified tag is removed until we check again.** Only our team can give the tag.

## 3. Post an ad (free)
Tap **Post an ad**.
1. **Job title** (for example "Plumbers needed"), **company**, **country**, **city**, **kind of work**.
2. **WhatsApp number**: job seekers will talk to you here. Write 10 digits (we add 91) or the full number with the country code.
3. **Salary and other facts** (optional but helpful): lowest and highest salary per month, the currency (AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, OMR, BHD, INR, USD), the experience you need, the number of vacancies, the last date to apply, the visa type. Ads with a salary get more replies.
4. **More about the job**: a short note (up to 600 letters).
5. **The ad itself**: add your **picture** (JPG, PNG or WebP) or a **PDF**. Pictures are made smaller automatically; a PDF can be up to 2 MB. This is what job seekers see, so make sure the company name and your phone number are clear. **Do not upload ID cards, passports or other personal papers.**
6. Tap **Send for approval**.

There is nothing to pay and no plan to choose. You can have up to 20 ads waiting for approval at a time.

## 4. Approval and going live
Our team reads every ad before it goes live, and chooses for how many days it stays live. You see the steps on **My ads**:

| What you see | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for approval** | our team is reading your ad | wait; you can **Delete** it if you change your mind |
| **Live until <date>** | visitors can see it and apply | **See ad**, **Ask us to change this** |
| **Rejected** | we found a problem; the reason is written there | **Fix and send again** |
| **Hidden** | we hid it (for example after a report) | contact us |
| **Expired** | the days are over; it is no longer shown | ask us to add days (it goes live again), or **Renew** to make a new ad copied from this one |

We reject ads that ask job seekers for money (visa fee, registration fee, "security deposit"), that have no company or phone number, that look fake, or that show personal documents. Fix what the reason says and send the ad again.

## 5. Applications
When a job seeker applies, you see their **name, phone and message** in **Applications** (and a WhatsApp button to answer). You see the names and phone numbers only after our team has **verified** your company. Until then you see only **how many** people applied. Applications are deleted after 180 days; please keep them private and use them only for this job.

## 6. Changes after approval
An approved ad cannot be edited by you (this protects job seekers). Open **My ads**, tap **Ask us to change this**, and write what to change (up to 3 open requests per ad). Our team makes the change and marks it done.

## 7. More days
When your ad's days are over it stops showing. Ask us (WhatsApp or **Ask us to change this**) and our team can add days; the ad then goes live again.

## 8. Rules you agree to
Post only real jobs. Do not take money from job seekers for a job, an interview or a visa unless the law allows it. Be honest about salary and work. If a job seeker reports your ad, we may hide it and block your account. A false or expired license means we remove your Verified tag and your ads.

## 9. Delete your account
Use **Delete my account** (link at the bottom of every page), write your email and our team will check and delete it within about 7 working days.

## Help
WhatsApp or email: see the **Contact** page. For complaints, the person named on the Contact page answers within 2 days.
