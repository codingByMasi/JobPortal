# 16 - Employer guide (plain words)

For recruitment agents and companies who want to show a job ad on **Jobs Abroad**. You can give this page to your employers as it is.

## What you get
Job seekers in India see your ad for free, without logging in, and apply to you by WhatsApp or by a short form. You pay only to show an ad. Looking at the site and making an account is free.

## 1. Make your account (free, 2 minutes)
1. Open the site and tap **Login**, then **Sign up**.
2. Write your **company name**, **phone number**, **registration number** (if you are a recruitment agent: this is very important, see "Verified" below), **email** and a **password** (at least 8 letters).
3. Tick the box to accept the **employer rules**. Tap **Create account**.
4. Open your email, find the message from us and tap the link to confirm. If you do not see it, look in the spam folder.
5. Now log in. You are on **My ads**.

Forgot your password? On the login page tap **Forgot password**, write your email and open the link we send. The link works once.

## 2. Post an ad
Tap **Post an ad**.
1. **Job title** (for example "Plumbers needed"), **company**, **country**, **city**, **kind of work**.
2. **WhatsApp number**: job seekers will talk to you here. Write 10 digits (we add 91) or the full number with the country code.
3. **Salary and other facts** (optional but helpful): lowest and highest salary per month, the currency (AED, SAR, QAR, KWD, OMR, BHD, INR, USD), the experience you need, the number of vacancies, the last date to apply, the visa type. Ads with a salary get more replies.
4. **More about the job**: a short note (up to 600 letters).
5. **The ad itself**: add your **picture** (JPG, PNG or WebP) or a **PDF**. Pictures are made smaller automatically; a PDF can be up to 2 MB. This is what job seekers see, so make sure the company name and your phone number are clear. **Do not upload ID cards, passports or other personal papers.**
6. **Choose a plan**: each card shows the price and for how many days your ad stays live. A *Featured* plan shows your ad at the top with a star.
7. **Coupon code** (if you have one): type it and tap **Apply**. The box "What you pay" shows the price, the discount, any extra days, and the total. If the code is wrong, expired or already used, we tell you in plain words. One offer is used per ad; offers do not add up.
8. Tap **Save and go to payment**.

You can keep up to 20 unpaid ads at a time.

## 3. Pay (Phase 1: by UPI)
After you save, the **payment screen** shows the amount, the **UPI ID**, the **name on the UPI ID** and your **ad code** (looks like `AD-1A2B3C4D`).
1. Open any UPI app (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). Check that the **name shown** matches the name on our screen.
2. Pay the exact amount. Write the ad code in the note.
3. Tap **I have paid, send details on WhatsApp** and send us your payment screenshot.
4. We check the payment in our bank, mark it paid, and your ad goes to approval. This can take a few hours. Payment details missing on the screen? We have not finished setting up: tap the WhatsApp button and we will help.
**Never pay anyone else or any other account.** We will never ask you for money outside this screen. If the total is zero (a free offer), no payment is needed: the ad goes straight to approval.

## 4. Approval and going live
Your ad goes through these steps (you see them on **My ads**):
| What you see | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Waiting for payment** | saved, not paid yet | **Pay now**, **Edit**, **Delete** |
| **Waiting for approval** | paid; our team is reading your ad | wait; you cannot edit now |
| **Live until <date>** | visitors can see it and apply | **See ad**, **Ask us to change this** |
| **Rejected** | we found a problem; the reason is written there | **Fix and send again** (no new payment) |
| **Hidden** | we hid it (for example after a report) | contact us |
| **Expired** | the days are over | **Renew**: starts a new ad copied from this one (new payment) |

We reject ads that ask job seekers for money (visa fee, registration fee, "security deposit"), that have no company or phone number, that look fake, or that show personal documents. Fix what the reason says and send the ad again.

## 5. Applications
When a job seeker applies, you see their **name, phone and message** in **Applications** (and a WhatsApp button to answer). You see the names and phone numbers only after our team has **verified** your company. Until then you see only **how many** people applied. Applications are deleted after 180 days; please keep them private and use them only for this job.

## 6. Changes after payment
A paid ad cannot be edited by you (this protects job seekers). Open **My ads**, tap **Ask us to change this**, and write what to change (up to 3 open requests per ad). Our team makes the change and marks it done.

## 7. Verified badge
A **Verified** tag with your **registration number** shows on your ads after our team has checked your company. They may phone you and check your registration. Keep your **My details** page up to date. **If you change the company name or the registration number, the tag is removed until we check again.** Only our team can give the tag.

## 8. My payments and receipts
**My payments** lists every payment. Open one to print a **receipt** (it is not a tax invoice; ask us if you need one).

## 9. Refunds (draft rule)
If we reject your ad, or you change your mind before our team approves it, tell us on WhatsApp within 7 days for a full refund. After your ad is live there is no refund, unless the mistake was ours. If you paid twice or the wrong amount, send us the payment reference and we return the extra amount. (This is a draft; the final rule is on the **Refund policy** page.)

## 10. Rules you agree to
Post only real jobs. Do not take money from job seekers for a job, an interview or a visa unless the law allows it. Be honest about salary and work. If a job seeker reports your ad, we may hide it and block your account.

## 11. Delete your account
Use **Delete my account** (link at the bottom of every page), write your email and our team will check and delete it within about 7 working days. Payment records are kept as the law requires.

## Help
WhatsApp or email: see the **Contact** page. For complaints, the person named on the Contact page answers within 2 days.
